# Ideal joint ZZ measurement with the shortened LP helper

- Task: T-20261008-013; owner: codex; date: 2026-10-08.
- Status: DONE; RV-20261008-013 APPROVED by claude, 2026-10-08.
- Evidence: PROVED under the hypotheses below; cross-review approved.

The previously constructed helper, at r=3, implements the joint measurement
of all s*l canonical Z1 Z2 products under ideal preparation and operations.
Every full physical readout record has a Kraus operator proportional to the
corresponding joint projector. Thus it reveals no additional logical
information and causes no additional disturbance within that eigenspace.
This is an ideal measurement lemma, not a fault-tolerance result.

## Setup and physical circuit

CHECK (scope): inherit the common unit minors and conventions of the
[augmentation](LP_ONE_ROW_AUGMENTATION_LEMMA.md) and
[shortening](LP_ONE_COORDINATE_SHORTENING_LEMMA.md) lemmas, restricting r=3.
The data is Q=LP(A,B); the helper is Q_h=Q''=LP(A'',B), with A''=A'iota.
The helper has q=s*l logical qubits and no residual logical sector. Its
entire Z image is generated by the independent operators

\[
P_{j,m}=\bar Z_{1,j,m}\bar Z_{2,j,m},\qquad
1\le j\le s,\quad 0\le m<\ell .
\tag{1}
\]

Write \(z_{h,j,m}\) for the binary supports of the helper Z representatives
from the shortening lemma. Work in the binary expansion for the physical
circuit. Let \(F=\mathbb B(\eta_1)\), with data rows and helper columns,
where \(\eta_1=\operatorname{diag}(\iota\otimes I_{s+b},p\otimes I_b)\).
It sends \(z_{h,j,m}\) to the support of (1), with the inherited positive signs.

For t=D,h define the X- and Z-stabilizer support spaces and the allowed
computational strings by

\[
S_X^t=\operatorname{im}(H_X^t)^{\mathsf T},\qquad
S_Z^t=\operatorname{im}(H_Z^t)^{\mathsf T},\qquad
C_t=(S_Z^t)^\perp=\ker H_Z^t.
\tag{2}
\]

All CSS stabilizers have eigenvalue +1. Put S=S_X^h. The exact helper
state with every logical Z equal to +1 is
\(\lvert0_L^q\rangle_h=|S|^{-1/2}\sum_{v\in S}\lvert v\rangle_h\).
This state is assumed available, not prepared by a circuit proved here.

Apply a CNOT from data qubit u to helper qubit v for each F_uv=1.
With disjoint data and helper registers, this circuit acts as
\(U_F\lvert x,v\rangle=\lvert x,v+F^{\mathsf T}x\rangle\).
Measure every helper qubit in Z, obtaining a string y. Helper coordinates
with zero columns in F are still prepared and read out; none is discarded
before the coupling. Preparation, CNOTs and readout are all noiseless,
with no unaccounted preparation Pauli frame.

## Lemma

PROVED (conditional): for an allowed record y in C_h, define the decoded
bits \(b_{j,m}(y)=z_{h,j,m}^{\mathsf T}y\). Set

\[
\Pi_{\mathbf b}=\prod_{j,m}\frac{I+(-1)^{b_{j,m}}P_{j,m}}{2}.
\tag{3}
\]

The fine-record Kraus operator \(K_y={}_h\langle y|U_F|0_L^q\rangle_h\),
restricted to the data codespace Q, is exactly

\[
K_y|_Q=
\begin{cases}
|S|^{-1/2}\Pi_{\mathbf b(y)}|_Q,&y\in C_h,\\
0,&y\notin C_h.
\end{cases}
\tag{4}
\]

There is no data Pauli byproduct in this fixed sign convention. Grouping
records by their decoded bits gives the unnormalized instrument

\[
\mathcal I_{\mathbf b}(\rho)=\Pi_{\mathbf b}\rho\Pi_{\mathbf b}
\tag{5}
\]

for arbitrary data states supported on Q. With an arbitrary reference E,
replace every projector by \(\Pi_{\mathbf b}\otimes I_E\).

## Proof from the full physical readout

The approved chain-map squares imply
\(F S_Z^h\subseteq S_Z^D\) and
\(F^{\mathsf T}S_X^D\subseteq S_X^h\).
The first inclusion implies \(F^{\mathsf T}C_D\subseteq C_h\): pair
F^T x with any helper Z stabilizer and move F onto that stabilizer.
The second inclusion ensures that data strings differing by an X
stabilizer produce the same helper coset modulo S.

The classes of the helper Z representatives form a complete basis of
\((S_X^h)^\perp/S_Z^h\). Consequently the parity map
\(L:C_h\to\mathbb F_2^q\), \(L(w)=(z_{h,j,m}^{\mathsf T}w)_{j,m}\),
has kernel exactly S. Indeed, a vector in its kernel is orthogonal both
to S_Z^h and to all the z_h,j,m, hence to the whole space (S_X^h)^perp,
and so lies in S. Conversely every vector of S has all these pairings zero.
Since \(\dim(C_h/S)=q\), L induces an isomorphism
\(C_h/S\cong\mathbb F_2^q\). Every fiber L^{-1}(b) has exactly |S|
strings, including all uncoupled helper coordinates.

Directly expanding the prepared helper gives, on a computational string x,

\[
K_y\lvert x\rangle
=|S|^{-1/2}\,\mathbf 1\{y+F^{\mathsf T}x\in S\}\lvert x\rangle.
\tag{6}
\]

All data codewords have computational support in C_D. For x in C_D,
F^T x lies in C_h, so y outside C_h makes (6) zero. For y in C_h, the
indicator in (6) equals one precisely when L(y)=L(F^T x). Its (j,m)
entry is \(z_{h,j,m}^{\mathsf T}F^{\mathsf T}x
=(Fz_{h,j,m})^{\mathsf T}x\), which is exactly the binary eigenvalue
label of P_j,m on x. Therefore the indicator is the joint projector (3).
Equation (6) proves (4) including amplitudes and signs, not just outcome
probabilities. Summing K_y rho K_y^dagger over the |S| strings in one fiber
cancels the factor |S|^{-1} and proves (5). The operator identity also
proves the extension to reference-entangled inputs. Independence of the
P_j,m makes the projectors a complete joint resolution of identity on Q.

## Information and disturbance

PROVED: \(p(y|\rho)=|S|^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(\Pi_{\mathbf b(y)}\rho)\)
for allowed y. Given a decoded result of nonzero probability, its |S|
fine records are uniform and independent of the remaining logical state.
More strongly, (4) assigns the same data operation to every such record.
All logical branches are retained; no postselection is required.

The instrument preserves coherence inside each joint eigenspace: it does
not separately measure Z1 or Z2. Both logical X3 and Z3, as well as X1 X2
at each (j,m), commute with every projector. Family 3 is therefore not
measured or directly disturbed. Conditioning can still update its reduced
state if it was correlated with the measured parities; this is ordinary
conditioning, not extra measurement leakage. Ignoring all outcomes gives
only the intended joint-parity dephasing \(\sum_{\mathbf b}\Pi_{\mathbf b}
\rho\Pi_{\mathbf b}\).

## Scope and source

PROVED: the result measures q=s*l independent products, not one chosen
(j,m). Forgetting other outcome bits does not undo their measurements.

OPEN: single-position addressability, general-r selective constructions,
rank-dropping or residual sectors, noisy preparation/coupling/readout,
distance preservation, fault tolerance, decoding performance and runtime.
Known nonzero preparation frames require explicit propagation and outcome
rules and are outside this fixed-state lemma. No claim of arbitrary
parallel PPMs or a native one-cycle implementation follows.
