### F4. Automorphism orientation ("convention correction"): the forced-duality argument is verified, not just checked

This is the most important claim for review request #2, and I could not
accept it on inspection of the arithmetic alone, so I derived it. Given only
two inputs sourced from the physical description (which I **cannot**
independently verify against the PDF): (a) the copy-swap automorphism acts on
the canonical Z-logical generators via the stated matrix `g`, and (b) `g` is
involutory, I show **by hand and then confirm numerically** that the
X-logical action is **forced** to be `g^T` (not a free convention choice): for
*any* computational-basis permutation `U|u> = |Au>`, elementary Pauli algebra
gives `U Z_i U^dagger = Z((A^-1)^T e_i)` and `U X_i U^dagger = X(A e_i)`
*unconditionally*. Requiring the first to equal the physically-stated
`Z(g e_i)` forces `A = g^T` (using `g^2=I`), and substituting into the second
immediately gives `X(g^T e_i)` — exactly the contract's claim, derived from
first principles rather than read off a reference.

I verified this numerically from scratch (own 16x16 dense Pauli/permutation
construction, not reusing `verification/lp_ghz.py`'s `dense_logical_audit`):
`g^2 = I` (confirmed); with `A=g^T`, `U Z_i U^dagger = Z(g e_i)` and
`U X_i U^dagger = X(g^T e_i)` for all `i`, residual 0.0; a genuine negative
control (`A=g` instead of `g^T`) gives the *same* `Z_i` target
`Z(g e_i)` a residual of 5.66 (large, i.e. the wrong orientation provably
fails the one relation that is sourced from the physical description); `U`
applied to `|000+>` reproduces the signed GHZ state exactly (residual 0.0, up
to the stated Pauli-frame correction); `U` applied to `|+000>` is an *exact*
fixed point (residual 0.0 to the original state, and far from either GHZ
branch) — confirming the contract's claim that `|+000>` does **not** prepare
GHZ under this physical automorphism.

### F5. Homomorphic coupling and the d_Z=1 fault witness: reproduced exactly

Independently verified, for the target range (physical qubits 3,150-3,674,
matching `INFO[3]*7*75` through `+525`, the "logical row i=4" block): the
restriction of every one of my 3,150 `H_Z` rows to those 525 columns lies in

empty (no X-checks at all), `d_Z=1` is immediate for *any* single Z, not a

with position 150 (fibre 2, i.e. `INFORMATION[0]*75`) giving exactly **1** —
matching the contract's named single-fault witness. (The flip-count is
constant within each 75-wide fibre, a cyclic-symmetry fact I confirmed
directly rather than assumed.)
