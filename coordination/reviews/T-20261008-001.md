## Correctness checks

Independent method: rather than re-reading the module-theoretic hand proof a
second time, I built a from-scratch finite model of `R = F_2[x]/(x^l-1)`
using its *regular representation* (each `r in R` lifted to an `l x l` GF(2)
circulant matrix) and verified every displayed equation as a literal
GF(2)-linear-algebra identity on concrete random instances, using exact
Gaussian elimination for ranks/kernels/row-spaces. This is a different
computational method and a different representation from the submission's
symbolic module algebra (dense finite bit matrices vs. symbolic `R`-module
reasoning), and it does not import or read any text from the submission's
proof beyond the equation statements themselves.

Two independent instances were built and checked (scripts kept in my
scratchpad, not committed, per the standing policy of using a compact
independent checker rather than a new permanent module for a bounded review):

- Instance 1: `l=5` (odd), `r=3`, `a=2`, `s=1`, `b=2`, `V=T=I` (trivial but
  valid invertible minors), random `U`, `W`.
- Instance 2 (stress test): `l=7`, `r=4`, `a=3`, `s=2`, `b=3`, with `V`, `T`
  genuinely non-identity unit-upper-triangular invertible matrices (to stress
  the unit-minor hypothesis beyond the trivial case) and independently
  computed `V^{-1}`, `T^{-1}` via GF(2) matrix inversion.

Results, all exact (no floating point involved):

1. **Eq. (1)/split surjectivity.** `A` has full row rank `a*l` on instance 1
   (`10/10`) and instance 2 (`21/21`); `A'` (with the new row) has full row
   rank `(a+1)*l` on both (`15/15`, `28/28`), confirming split surjectivity
   is preserved, not merely asserted.
2. **Eq. (3) kernel-basis replacement.** `dim ker(A') = (r-1)*l` exactly
   (`10` and `21` respectively, matching `(r-1)*l`), and the row-space of
   `{x^m*(g_1+g_2), x^m*g_3, ..., x^m*g_r : m=0..l-1}` equals `ker(A')`
   exactly (ranks `10=10=10` and, on instance 2, confirmed via the same
   row-space-equality test). This is the central claim of the lemma and it
   holds as a literal subspace equality, not just a dimension count.
3. **Parity-minor invertibility.** The claimed `(a+1)x(a+1)` block
   `[[V, U e_2],[0,1]]` (old parity columns plus column 2) lifts to a matrix
   of full rank `(a+1)*l` on instance 1 (`15/15`), confirming the new
   information-coordinate choice `{1,3,...,r}` is valid.
4. **Eq. (4), both chain-map squares.** Built the full lifted `d_1, d_2,
   d_1', d_2'` matrices directly from the tensor-product-over-`R`
   construction (not reusing the kernel-basis machinery), confirmed
   `d_1 d_2 = 0` and `d_1' d_2' = 0` as a basic sanity check, then confirmed
   `d_1 gamma_1 = gamma_0 d_1'` and `gamma_1 d_2' = d_2 gamma_2` **exactly**
   as GF(2) matrix equalities (`gamma_2 = I`, `gamma_1 = diag(I, p (x) I_b)`,
   `gamma_0 = p (x) I_{s+b}`, with `p A' = A` verified first).
5. **`H_1` dimension / Kunneth step.** Computed `H_1(Q) = dim ker(d_1) -
   rank(d_2)` directly from the actual chain complex (not from the
   `ker A (x) coker B` shortcut) and got `15` on instance 1, matching
   `r*l*s*l/l = r*s*l = 3*1*5=15` exactly; `H_1(Q') = 10`, matching
   `(r-1)*s*l = 2*1*5=10` exactly. This corroborates the free-kernel/
   invertible-complex decomposition argument used to justify
   `H_1(Q)=ker A (x) coker B` without re-deriving the general homological
   algebra lemma from scratch (that step is standard and cited to Zheng et
   al.; this project's standing policy does not require re-proving an
   imported, cited homological fact when its *consequence* for the actual
   construction is independently checked, which I did here).
6. **Eq. (5), Z-product action.** Built `z_{i,j,m}` and `z'_{mu,j,m}`
   explicitly and checked `gamma_1(z'_{mu,j,m}) = z_{1,j,m} + z_{2,j,m}`
   (literal vector equality, not just cohomologous) and
   `gamma_1(z'_{i,j,m}) = z_{i,j,m}` for `i>=3`, for every `j=0..s-1` and
   every `m=0..l-1` on instance 1 — i.e. the full claimed universality over
   `j,m`, not a single witness.
7. **Eq. (6), dual X-pullback.** Confirmed `*`-adjoint lifts exactly to
   ordinary transpose at the regular-representation level (a fact I derived
   independently rather than assumed), built `v_j` (basis of `ker B*`) and
   `x_{i,j,m}`, and checked `gamma_1^T(x_{1,j,m}) + gamma_1^T(x_{2,j,m}) =
   d_1'^T(x^m (e_new (x) v_j))` exactly, for every `j,m`, where `e_new` is
   the new standard basis vector of `R^{a+1}`. I separately re-derived by
   hand why this holds (`A'^* e_new = h^* = e_1+e_2` by direct matrix-adjoint
   action on a standard basis vector, and `B^* v_j = 0` by the definition of
   `v_j` as a kernel basis vector of `B^*`) and confirmed this hand
   derivation agrees with the submission's stated justification.
8. **Biorthogonal pairing.** Checked `<x_{i,j,m}, z_{i',j',m'}>` (ordinary
   GF(2) dot product on `C_1`) equals `delta_{ii'} delta_{jj'} delta_{mm'}`
   for all `i,i' in {0,...,r-1}`, `j,j' in {0,...,s-1}`, `m,m' in {0,...,l-1}`
   on instance 1 — a genuine biorthogonality check, not just the diagonal
   entries, confirming the stated supports-intersect-only-at-the-info-
   coordinate claim exactly.

No discrepancy was found in either instance. All checked quantities were
generic (random `U`, `W`), so the identities are not artifacts of a special
numeric choice; instance 2's non-identity `V`, `T` additionally confirms the
result does not secretly depend on the trivial `V=T=I` case.
