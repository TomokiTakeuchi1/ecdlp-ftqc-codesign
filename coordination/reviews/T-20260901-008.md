## Non-blocking observation

`_short_condition` strips parentheses from Boolean conditions for display
(e.g. `m_x_0 XOR (m_zz_1 AND m_zz_2)` renders as `m_x_0 xor m_zz_1 and
m_zz_2`), relying on the reader to know AND binds tighter than XOR in this
GF(2) convention. The contract already discloses that "visible
observable/condition strings are compact labels; canonical full data remain
in `atlas.json` and SVG `data-*` metadata," so this is not a hidden gap, only
a possible future legibility improvement (e.g. keeping parentheses in the
short form) if a future task revisits the visual grammar.
