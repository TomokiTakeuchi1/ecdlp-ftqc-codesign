## Findings

Scientific:

- **Pixel-level source verification (new for this review).** Rendered PDF
  page 20 of `literature/papers/2211.15465v1.pdf` to a 600 DPI PNG (via
  `pdftoppm`, since the Read tool reported the file as password-protected but
  `pdftotext`/`pdftoppm` opened it fine) and visually inspected Figure 14(a)
  directly, cropped to full legibility. Confirmed every wire assignment by
  eye:
  - The three `ZZ` measurements pair each data qubit with the
    same-index resource qubit: `a = ZZ(c1,CCZ1)`, `b = ZZ(c2,CCZ2)`,
    `c = ZZ(t,CCZ3)` -- matches `operand_roles: [resource_i, data_i]` in the
    capability YAML exactly.
  - The three dashed conditional-CZ boxes connect exactly: box `a` -> dots at
    rows (c2, t) = `CZ(c2,t)`; box `b` -> dots at rows (c1, t) = `CZ(c1,t)`;
    box `c` -> dots at rows (c1, c2) = `CZ(c1,c2)` -- matches
    `CZ(data_1,data_2)^{zz_0}`, `CZ(data_0,data_2)^{zz_1}`,
    `CZ(data_0,data_1)^{zz_2}` exactly (data_0=c1, data_1=c2, data_2=t).
  - The final correction layer shows `Z` on c1 controlled by a double-line
    labeled `b^c` that threads through the `X`-measurement box on `CCZ1`
    (i.e. the control bit is `x_0 XOR (b AND c)`), `Z` on c2 controlled by
    `a^c` threaded through `CCZ2`'s `X` box (`x_1 XOR (a AND c)`), and `X`
    (not `Z`, correctly after the second target Hadamard) on `t` controlled
    by `a^b` threaded through `CCZ3`'s `X` box (`x_2 XOR (a AND b)`) --
    matches `Z(data_0)^({x_0} XOR ({zz_1} AND {zz_2}))`,
    `Z(data_1)^({x_1} XOR ({zz_0} AND {zz_2}))`,
    `X(data_2)^({x_2} XOR ({zz_0} AND {zz_1}))` exactly.
  - Confirmed the target Hadamard appears exactly twice, once before the `ZZ`
    measurements and once after the conditional CZs and before the resource
    `X` measurements, matching the "target correction is `X`, not `Z`"
    convention note in the contract.
  - Every element of `DIRECT_CCZ_FIGURE14_CORRECTION_MAP` (in
    `src/ecdlp_ftqc/architectures/direct_ccz.py`) and the identical
    `exact_boolean_map` in
    `configs/architectures/direct_ccz_single_event_capability_v1.yaml` is a
