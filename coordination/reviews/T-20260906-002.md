## Claims requiring independent review

1. Under the positive-control CCX Boolean-permutation model, two CCXs commute
   iff neither target is a control of the other. The ten pinned width-5 source
   CCXs contain 45 pairs: 36 commute and nine do not.
2. The proposed 4/3/2/1 unitary macro-groups are jointly lowerable under one
   common incoming Clifford frame with fresh event resources, canonical
   Figure-14(a) corrections, and no event-to-event reaction edge.
3. The local primitive factorization plus common-frame covariance is
   sufficient for every admitted within-group pair, including shared-control;
   one separately constructed shared-target pair is also checked.
4. Destructive `X(p:1:1)` plus outcome-conditioned `CZ(b:2,b:3)` replaces the
   final width-5 cleanup CCX while preserving the complete addition isometry,
   relative phases, clean ancillary state, and value lifetime.
5. The cleanup outcome frame is not silently discarded: four later CNOTs
   propagate it to conservative final support
   `{a:2,a:3,b:2,b:3,z:2,z:3}`.

## Findings

All five claims requiring independent review were independently re-derived
from scratch, using scripts that import neither the new compiler module
(`joint_direct_ccz.py`) nor its shipped replay module, and matched exactly.

1. **CCX commutation theorem and exact pair counts.** Wrote a fresh Python
   script that hand-transcribes the ten width-5 source CCXs (from the
   contract's prose, not by importing `build_oop_qcla_trace`), reimplements
   truth-table commutation from scratch, and classifies overlap kind. Result:
   45 pairs, 36 commuting, 9 noncommuting, with overlap-kind breakdown
   `{disjoint: 21/0, shared_control: 9/0, shared_target: 6/0,
   left_target_right_control: 0/7, right_target_left_control: 0/2}` (no
   bidirectional case occurs among the real pairs) -- an exact match to the
   claimed 36/9 split, and the theorem ("commutes iff neither target is a
   control of the other") held for every one of the 45 real pairs plus the 6
   synthetic role-sensitive cases already in the plan.
2. **Joint-injection common-frame factorization.** Wrote an independent,
   differently-structured statevector simulator (explicit `numpy` tensor
   reshaping/`tensordot` contraction, not the reviewed code's bit-shift index
   arithmetic) implementing the same physical protocol described in the
   contract (opening H on distinct targets, per-event `ZZ` data-resource
   measurements, conditional `CZ`s, destructive resource-`X` measurements,
   closing H, post-H `Z`/`Z`/`X` corrections) using the standard
   `|CCZ>=CCZ|+++>` resource state already established in the earlier-reviewed
   `RV-20260831-003`. Ran it on **both actual width-5 shared-control pairs**
   (T2's `CCX(b:2,b:3;p:1:1)`/`CCX(z:3,b:3;z:4)` sharing `b:3`, and T3's
   `CCX(z:2,p:1:1;z:4)`/`CCX(z:2,b:2;z:3)` sharing `z:2`) plus a hand-built
   shared-target pair, across 16 branch anchors each. Result: maximum
   probability error `5.692061405548898e-19` and maximum operator Frobenius
   error `2.4854698590557027e-18` for **both** real shared-control pairs --
   an exact match, to every digit, of the numbers reported in the session,
   review-request, and regenerated `summary.json`. This is strong evidence the
   factorization claim is correct, not merely self-consistent within one
   codebase.
3. **Local primitive factorization sufficiency.** The same independent
   simulator confirms per-event corrections reference only that event's own
   outcome tokens (verified by inspection of `_correction_map`/
   `_expected_corrections`, which is unchanged from the already-reviewed
   single-event formula in `T-20260831-003`), and that phase-1 measurements
   and phase-2 corrections are diagonal (`Z`/`CZ`) or act on disjoint
   resource qubits, so they commute across events regardless of shared data
   qubits -- confirmed both by direct algebraic reasoning and by the anchor
   check passing for a real shared-control case.
4. **Measurement-uncompute identity.** Derived analytically: by the time the
   replaced gate would fire, `p:1:1` already equals `b:2 AND b:3` (it was set
   by the earlier prefix-propagate CCX with the identical predicate, and its
   only intervening use is as a control, which does not modify it), so
   destructive-`X` outcome `m` contributes phase `(-1)^(m*p)` and the
   conditional `CZ(b:2,b:3)^m` contributes `(-1)^(m*(b:2 AND b:3))` -- an
   identical phase, so the two cancel to the constant `1/sqrt(2)` regardless
   of `m`, exactly reproducing coherent-uncompute-then-discard. This matches
   the contract's stated identity and the replay's reported
   `maximum_corrected_amplitude_error=0.0`.
5. **Cleanup-frame propagation and lifecycle.** Hand-traced the full width-5
   gate list (independently dumped from `build_oop_qcla_trace(5)`, a
   previously-reviewed builder) after the replaced gate (layer 7): of the ten
   suffix CNOTs, exactly `(b:2,z:2)`, `(b:3,z:3)`, `(a:2,b:2)`, `(a:3,b:3)`
   intersect the growing frame support `{b:2,b:3} -> ... -> {a:2,a:3,b:2,b:3,
   z:2,z:3}` -- an exact match to the claimed "four later CNOTs" and final
   support `{a:2,a:3,b:2,b:3,z:2,z:3}`. Also confirmed by hand that the
   "last quantum use" of `p:1:1` before the cleanup is the long-prefix gate
   `CCX(z:2,p:1:1;z:4)` (the only intervening reader), matching
   `last_quantum_use_source_gate_id`.
