- Source identity spot-checked against arXiv directly for the two recommended baselines: `arXiv:2606.02235` (Schrottenloher, single version, submitted 2026-06-01) and `arXiv:2607.13816` (Luo et al., v1 2026-07-15, v2 2026-08-09, "supersedes arXiv:2604.02311") both match the memo's title, author list, and version claims exactly.

The memo's `CHECK` label on `2^30.63` and `NUMERICAL` label on the recomputation are correctly assigned.
- The selection gates (public generator, recoverable dependencies at more than one level, explicit non-Clifford counting convention, measurement/reset/classical-condition recoverability) correctly reject count-only candidates (Babbush et al.) rather than treating a competitive scalar Toffoli/qubit number as sufficient.
- The memo keeps the Table-1 arithmetic-core count, the window/lookup addition, and the full 28-addition Shor estimate in three separate rows instead of collapsing them, matching `CONVENTIONS.md`'s scope-separation requirement.
- The approximate-arithmetic/random-input failure semantics and the exceptional-input (`i=0`) handling are stated as explicit trace-schema obligations rather than left implicit, so a later schema implementer cannot silently assume exact arithmetic.
- No claim states or implies a published count was reproduced; the "Local verification status" section correctly downgrades both timed-out artifact runs to `INCONCLUSIVE` rather than treating a non-crash as a pass.
- Gate-count aggregation risk (CCX/CCZ/AND treated as one paper-level "Toffoli") is flagged as a schema-preservation requirement, consistent with the project's ban on hiding logical-operation identity behind one scalar metric.
- No evidence found of algorithmic parallelism (e.g., generator block structure) being silently reinterpreted as executable concurrency; the memo explicitly withholds a depth/schedule claim.
