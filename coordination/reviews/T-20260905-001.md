**Defect found — citation/evidence-label mismatch for the `one_bit_h_teleportation` execution option (reviewer-requested check, item 6 above regarding H specifically):**

The contract (`docs/HIGH_RATE_QCLA_H_CNOT_STRATEGY_CONTRACT.md`), the `one_bit_h_teleportation` `ExecutionOption` record in `src/ecdlp_ftqc/analysis/qcla_clifford_strategy.py:415-434`, and assumption `A-0042` all cite "Xu et al. 2024, arXiv:2407.18490v1, Figure 11" as the source for a one-bit H-teleportation gadget using a provisioned `|0>` output carrier with ordered `ZX` then destructive `X` measurements.

I rendered Xu et al. page 28 (Figure 11) at 300 DPI and inspected it directly. Its caption reads "FIG. 11. Circuits for implementing (a) teleportation (b) teleported S gate (c) measurement-based CNOT." There is no H-teleportation panel:

- Panel (a) is generic identity teleportation: joint `ZZ` measurement between the input and a `|+>` ancilla, then destructive `X` measurement of the input, with `X`/`Z` corrections on the ancilla.
- Panel (b) is a teleported S gate: `CNOT` from input to a `|i>` ancilla, then a single-qubit `Z` measurement on the input, with a `Y` correction.
- Panel (c) is the measurement-based CNOT already correctly cited elsewhere in this same record set (finding 7 above).

Xu et al.'s actual method for logical Hadamards (their Section 5, "Parallel logical Hadamard gates") is a different, global technique: a fold-transversal H-SWAP gate followed by swap-correction using the generic panel-(a) teleportation primitive repeatedly — not a single-qubit `ZX`/`X` gadget with a `|0>` ancilla.

I then independently checked whether the *specific* `ZX`-then-`X`, `|0>`-ancilla construction described in the contract is nonetheless a mathematically valid H-teleportation gadget in its own right (i.e., whether this is a pure citation slip rather than a physics error). A from-scratch numpy simulation (20 random test states, exact statevector arithmetic) confirms it is: for all four `(a,b)` outcome branches, a single deterministic Pauli correction (`I`, `X`, `Z`, or `Y` respectively) recovers `H|psi>` with fidelity `1.0` to floating-point precision. So the gadget itself is correct and internally consistent with the module's own claims (matching `added_ppm_axes=("ZX","X")` and the `|0>` output-carrier description) — this is a sourcing/evidence-label defect, not a computational-correctness defect. (For contrast, I first tried the literal Figure 11(a) circuit — `ZZ` measurement with a `|0>` ancilla substituted for `|+>` — which does *not* implement H, max overlap `0.765613`; that confirms the figure's actual circuit is unrelated to the claimed gadget, rather than merely being an unfamiliar notation for the same thing.)

Per `AGENTS.md`'s evidence-label rules, `LITERATURE` asserts the claim is established by the cited primary source. Here it is not: Xu Figure 11 does not contain this gadget, and the paper's own stated H method is categorically different (global transversal, not one-bit teleportation). The claim should be relabeled (e.g. `NUMERICAL`, since it is now independently machine-verified in this review and could be re-verified by an in-repo channel test) and either re-cited to its actual origin (this exact one-ancilla-teleported-Hadamard construction is a known measurement-based-computation/lattice-surgery technique; possible sources include Litinski's PBC gadget literature, not yet checked here) or presented as self-derived, with Figure 11's citation retained only for the already-correct CNOT gadget and the generic teleportation primitive it depends on.
