### F1. Legacy (conjugated-H, bare-Clifford) semantics: derived and verified densely

- Derivation: `physical = F ideal`. A bare source Clifford `U` gives `F -> U F U^dagger`.
  With both physical H layers `G = F H_T F^dagger` at the entrance frame, injection
  gives `F D V H_T`, and the closing `G` leaves `F_out = F H_T D H_T` (the approved
  compiler's `F_in (H D H)`).
- Independent dense simulation (from scratch, not the repository's): arbitrary incoming
  real Clifford frame, shared-control and shared-target two-event macros, physical
  opening `G`, `F Z F^dagger (x) Z_res` PPMs, resource X readout, closing `G`, all 4,096
  outcome branches x 2 frames. Result `raw = scalar * F_out * prod(CCX)`, residual
  <= 2.2e-16, |scalar| = 1/64. The alternative policy the contract warns about (closing
  `G` conjugated by the post-correction frame `F D`) **fails** (residual 8.8e-2, scalar
  vanishing on some branches), so the contract's "different policy" statement is correct
  and the check discriminates. Bare `U` semantics verified (residual 0).
- The Z-family theorem is sound: admitted macro targets are never same-macro controls,
  so `H_T D H_T` stays in the `CNOT/CZ/X/Z` group, which maps Z-type to Z-type up to
  sign. Empirically every legacy PPM in all checked traces has `x = 0`.

### F5. Non-blocking notes

1. **The budget is an accounting identity; what it does not decide is large.** Because both
   QWC lowerings have zero extra preparation stages and equal readout/reaction, `B_crit`
   equals surgery-count difference times the surgery duration (as the contract proves), so
   the medians 29-219 units say only how many surgery steps the legacy Cliffords may cost.
   For orientation only: the reviewed T-20260909 study of the same physical policy (full
   conjugation, IP) reports a median logical envelope of 4,052 CNOT batches / 4,114
   layers; even a per-batch cost of roughly 0.05 surgery units would exceed the IP median
   `B_crit`. That literal-generator envelope is not native cost, and this task rightly
   prices `B` as unknown, but a one-line cross-reference would help a reader see that
   `B_crit` is not a knife-edge. (Suggestion, not a required change.)
2. **Selector blind spot is disclosed but worth keeping visible.** Cells that are 64/64
   both-feasible are not refined even if near a cap; the IP weight-512 case is fine (see
   F3), OOP has no 512-hash data. No unselected cell is claimed.
3. **Cohort mixing.** The QWC (218) and general (179) refined medians are on different
   branch sets; the contract says so twice. Keep that wording when quoting.
4. **Environment (unchanged from earlier reviews).** `mypy --strict` needs
   `--python-version 3.12` with the installed NumPy stubs; the pytest default temp root was
   inaccessible in this session (`--basetemp` used).
