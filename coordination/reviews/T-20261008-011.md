### F1. Analytic check (primary evidence)

Derived independently from the stated conventions, without reusing the
note's sentences:

1. **Eq. (2).** Every `g_i` carries `e_i` in its information block, so the
   coordinate-3 entry of `t_mu(g1+g2) + sum_{i>=3} t_i g_i` is exactly `t_3`
   (`g1`, `g2`, `g_i` for `i>=4` have zero there). `iota(ker A'')` is exactly
   `{u in ker A' : u_3 = 0}` because `A'' w = A'(iota w)` and `iota` is
   injective with image `{u_3 = 0}`. Hence (2) is a literal submodule
   equality, not a dimension count. Uniqueness of the expression comes from
   the approved predecessor's free basis.
2. **Unit minor.** Column 3 lies outside the minor `[[V, U e_2],[0,1]]`
   (old parity columns plus column 2), so the minor survives unchanged.
   `A''` is split surjective with information coordinates `{1,4,...,r}` and
   free kernel rank `(n-1)-(a+1) = r-2`, so the predecessor's
   contractible-summand argument applies verbatim (no residual sector).
3. **Eq. (3).** `A iota = p A' iota = p A''`. With the stated `eta`,
   `d1 eta1 = ((A iota) (x) I, (I (x) B)(p (x) I)) = ((A iota) (x) I, p (x) B)`
   and `eta0 d1'' = ((p A'') (x) I, p (x) B)`; `eta1 d2'' =
   (iota (x) B; (p A'') (x) I)` and `d2 eta2 = (iota (x) B; (A iota) (x) I)`.
   Both squares commute; domains/codomains match blockwise.
4. **Eq. (4) and "entire image".** `eta1` sends `(rho(g1+g2) (x) q_j, 0)` to
   `((g1+g2) (x) q_j, 0)` because `iota rho` fixes vectors with zero third
   coordinate. The induced homology map is the inclusion of the direct
   summand `ker A''` (via `iota`) tensored with the free `coker B`, hence
   injective, and its image is spanned by the merged and `i>=4` families
   only. So no class with a nonzero Z3 coefficient is in the image, which is
   the claimed full image statement.
5. **Eqs. (5)-(6).** `eta1^* = diag(rho (x) I, p^* (x) I)`; the old X
   representatives live in the first summand, so `eta1^* x_i = (rho e_i (x)
   v_j, 0)`: literally `x''_mu` for `i=1`, literally zero for `i=3`, and the
   new representative for `i>=4`. `d1''^*(e_new (x) v_j) = (A''^* e_new (x)
   v_j, e_new (x) B^* v_j) = (rho h^* (x) v_j, 0) = (rho(e1+e2) (x) v_j, 0)`,
   which is (5). With the literal `i=1` identity this gives (6) for `X2`.
   Note that, unlike the predecessor note, this note states the literal
   `i=1` equality explicitly, which resolves my earlier non-blocking
   presentation remark.
6. **Counts and boundary.** `k'' = (r-2) s l`, a drop of `s l` from
   `k' = (r-1) s l`; one R coordinate is a whole cyclic fibre of `l` binary
   positions, so `s l` binary logical qubits disappear from the helper, not
   one. At `r=3` the spectator sum is empty and the image is the merged
   family alone; nothing in the argument needs `r>=4`.
