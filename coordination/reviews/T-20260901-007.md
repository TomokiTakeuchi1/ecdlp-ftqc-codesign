## Implementer claims

- The exact symbolic symplectic commutator is the ANF XOR of per-index
  `x_left*z_right XOR z_left*x_right` polynomials. Per-index conflicts are
  recorded separately, so globally commuting `XX`/`ZZ`-type pairs cannot hide
  cancelling local axis conflicts.
- Branch-possible Y content is the exact per-index ANF product `x*z`. The 60
  width-5 presented PPMs have zero such Y content.
- Logical overlap alone is not a split rule. Six compatible width-5 pairs
  share logical support under the exact same-axis screen.
- The complete semantic DAG is rebuilt from operation predecessors, outcome
  dependencies, and completion barriers. Of 1,770 PPM pairs, 1,206 are causal
  and 1,116 are connected through a reaction update.
- Exact maximum compatible subsets are selected within each ready frontier.
  The optimized batch sizes are `12, 12, 6, 6, 3, 3, 3, 3, 3, 3, 3, 3`.
- Both the causal-path and conflict-clique lower bounds are 12 candidate
  levels. The 12-level construction is therefore optimal only inside the
  documented general logical candidate model.
- Under the explicit semantic-layer/just-in-time-clean liveness coordinate,
  the candidate construction peaks at 26 logical values and four live CCZ
  resource states; maximum presented PPM weight is seven. These are not
  physical-qubit, placement, memory, or capacity results.
- General candidates, finite admission, and low-rate fallback are separate
  schemas. The finite artifact contains `schedule=null`, `runtime_seconds=null`,
  `physical_qubits=null`, and `failure_probability=null`.
- The finite rejection preserves 13 capability gaps, 13 finite-backend gaps,
  three incompatible bb18 factory values, and four absent native-mapper
  obligations. It does not borrow J300 XX gadgets, T ports, or incompatible
  factory values.
- The low-rate artifact contains all 60 PPMs once in canonical topological
  order with every reaction ancestor recorded and finite cost `OPEN`.
- Measured-product rewrites and selective Clifford materialization are
  screened but have zero admitted transforms because their semantic/lifetime
  certificates and finite costs are absent.
- The independent replay imports neither the symbolic compiler nor the
  scheduler and reconstructs polynomial algebra, all profiles and pair
  relations, the causal/reaction DAG, liveness, both lower bounds, both
  candidate schedules, finite field lists/null outputs, fallback, and strategy
  frontier from serialized dictionaries.
