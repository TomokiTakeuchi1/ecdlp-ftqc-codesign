## Findings

Scientific:

- **Verified the single most important claim directly against the primary
  source text (not the note file's transcription of it).** Extracted Cain
  et al. pages 19 and 24 with `pdftotext`. Page 19 reports the exact
  `kf = 10 |CCZ> states ... p_L^CCZ ~ 10^-10 ... 120 ... cycles` figures and
  then states, in the very next sentence: *"in our space-efficient or
  balanced architecture, where we consume these |CCZ> states sequentially,
  we will neglect the time cost..."* -- i.e. the source text itself labels
  these numbers as belonging to the space-efficient/balanced regime, not the
  candidate's time-efficient `P=20` regime. Page 24 (Appendix F, the
  time-efficient architecture housing the `P=20` candidate) states
  *"Our goal is not to specify concrete circuits or instruction sets, but to
  provide an approximate resource estimate..."* This directly and literally
  confirms both halves of the audit's central claim: the finite factory

  candidate, and the candidate's own section explicitly disclaims giving a
  concrete construction (which is why the other 13 fields are correctly

  compatibility-domain rule is neither too broad (it requires exact
