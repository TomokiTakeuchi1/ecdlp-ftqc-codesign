## Reviewer findings

This task adds an entirely new analysis module
(`src/ecdlp_ftqc/scheduler/direct_ccz_ppm_scaling.py` and its independent
replay) rather than modifying any reviewed compiler/architecture/scheduler
code. `git diff --stat` confirms only `scheduler/__init__.py` and
`verification/__init__.py` changed among existing files, and both diffs are
purely additive export lists — the underlying primitives
(`qcla_metrics`/`oop_qcla_metrics`, `source_ancilla_count`,
`expected_fully_resident_source_values`, `topological_order`,
`event_lowering_admitted`, `FINITE_NATIVE_MAPPER_OBLIGATIONS`, the direct-CCZ
event adapters, `analyze_direct_ccz_ppm_batches`) are all read-only reused
from already-reviewed tasks, confirmed by grepping for their definitions
before trusting the new module's calls into them. This substantially bounds
the risk surface compared to the prior two reviews in this line.

1. **Weighted causal lower bound — general correctness argument, not just
   width-5/8 curve-fitting.** Independently confirmed the "weight two per
   Toffoli, zero per Clifford, longest source-DAG path" rule is a valid
   general lower bound: each admitted Figure-14(a) event has a strict internal
   causal chain of (3 ZZ PPMs) → (conditional corrections) → (3 resource-X
   PPMs), i.e. at least 2 sequentially-ordered PPM levels per Toffoli, and this
   chain composes correctly across dependent Toffolis. This is the same
   causal structure already reviewed and matrix-verified in
   `T-20260901-006`/`RV-20260901-005`, not a new physical claim. The reviewed
   module does not merely assert this — `analyze_qcla_direct_ccz_exact_anchor`
   actively cross-validates it by running BOTH the cheap weighted-path formula
   and the full expensive exact symbolic/ANF causal-path computation at widths
   5 and 8, and raises `DirectCczPpmScalingError` if they disagree (confirmed
   by reading the `if schedule.causal_critical_path_lower_bound !=
   structural.weighted_causal_ppm_level_lower_bound: raise` guard, which is
   exercised, not dead code, by both the shipped tests and the generator
   script's own anchor loop).
2. **Independent from-scratch verification of every headline number,
   including width 256.** Wrote a verification script that imports only
   `ecdlp_ftqc.circuits` (the source-trace builders, already reviewed in
   `T-20260824-003`/`T-20260831-002`) — never
   `ecdlp_ftqc.scheduler.direct_ccz_ppm_scaling`, `ecdlp_ftqc.compiler`, or
   `ecdlp_ftqc.verification` — and implemented its own weighted longest-path
   algorithm via Kahn's algorithm (a different topological-sort strategy than
   the reviewed module's `trace.topological_order()`-based DP). Rebuilt seven
   widths spanning the full range (5, 8, 17, 33, 65, 129, 256) completely from
   scratch and independently reproduced, with zero mismatches:
   - width 5: 23/43 gates, 10/20 Toffolis, 60/120 PPMs, causal bounds 12/24;
   - width 256: 1,229/2,458 events, 7,374/14,748 PPMs, causal bounds 34/68,
     theoretical pair counts 27,184,251/108,744,378 (computed via
     `P(P-1)/2` on the independently-derived PPM counts), and peak
     per-source-layer demand 255 events / 1,530 PPMs for both variants;
   - the IP-exactly-double-OOP relation (event count, non-Clifford
     source-layer count, causal lower bound) held at every one of the 7
     independently rebuilt widths, not just the widths the implementer
     highlighted.
3. **Power-boundary pattern sanity check.** Independently inspected
   `width_sweep.json`'s six power-of-two boundary windows and confirmed the
   claimed pattern (causal lower bound unchanged from `2^k-1` to `2^k`, then
   rising at `2^k+1`) holds at all six boundaries (8/16/32/64/128/256). This
   matches standard parallel-prefix carry-lookahead-network theory (prefix
   depth is `ceil(log2(n))`, constant on `(2^(k-1), 2^k]` and stepping at
   `2^k+1`), so the reported pattern is not an artifact but a real structural
   property of the already-reviewed Draper QCLA prefix network.
4. **Fail-closed finite gate.** `assess_qcla_direct_ccz_finite_scaling_admission`

   `capability.high_rate_schedule_admitted`, `backend_audit.candidate.backend_admitted`,

   self-checks (`if admitted: raise`) that it cannot possibly return a
   positive admission verdict under the current backend — a defensive
   assertion against a future silent capability/backend change accidentally

   Confirmed all ten native-output fields are hardcoded `None` dataclass

   `routing_cost`, `reaction_latency_seconds`, `decoder_service`,
   `ccz_buffer_trajectory`, `runtime_seconds`, `physical_qubits`,

5. **Storage-metric kind separation.** Confirmed OOP reports
